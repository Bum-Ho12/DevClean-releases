# Support

- Read **Help > How DevClean Works** in the app.
- Found a bug or want a feature? [Open an issue](https://github.com/Bum-Ho12/DevClean-releases/issues). Include your macOS version and the DevClean version (shown next to the title).
- Nothing was deleted that you didn't tick and confirm. If something you cleaned won't rebuild, tell us which row it was.
